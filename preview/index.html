Rules

do

  • Dark-first — ink-950 canvas, blue as the light source
  • One gradient moment per screen section
  • Let the sparkline draw itself on reveal — it's the signature motion
  • Keep numbers honest — the chart IS the aesthetic
  • Swap wordmarks freely; the mark is sacred

don't

  • Use the word "wrapped" — trademark line
  • Recolor or outline the sparkline
  • Gradient wallpaper — chroma is a highlight, not a background
  • Pure gray neutrals — ink is blue-tinted on purpose
  • Stack glows on the primary CTA and everything else
